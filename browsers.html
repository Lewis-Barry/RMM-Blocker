      Is this perfect?

      No. This policy matches browsers by expected file name and path, not by file hash or publisher signature. Renamed copies or browsers run from unexpected folders can get past it, and other apps in matching folders may be blocked.

      Many browsers share the Chromium chrome.exe name. That rule belongs to Google Chrome, so excluding another Chromium browser that uses it does not unblock it unless you also exclude Chrome.

      Treat this as one layer and test on pilot devices first.
